# B2B SaaS Customer Churn & Revenue Intelligence — SQL Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a B2B SaaS company's customers, subscriptions, revenue, payments, product usage, and support activity using SQL.

The goal is to identify customer churn patterns, revenue drivers, high-value customers, payment issues, and customers at risk of leaving.

The analysis converts raw business data into actionable business insights for customer retention and revenue growth.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and segments
- Understand subscription and plan performance
- Analyze revenue and payment activity
- Identify high-value customers
- Analyze customer churn and cancellations
- Identify customers with payment issues
- Analyze product usage
- Identify customers with higher support activity
- Categorize customers based on business risk
- Generate actionable business insights
